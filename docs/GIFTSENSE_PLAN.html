Summary

Features14 features, one release

Gift finder, notes, wrap and greeting cards, delivery dates, voice and video messages, registries and analytics. Recipient's choice comes after launch.

Plans3 plans: $19 · $49 · $99

Suggested prices. Every limit is a setting, so prices and limits can change later without code changes.

Worst-case profit84% – 91% per merchant

Even if a merchant uses 100% of every AI limit, every month. Normal usage will be much higher.

TimelineAbout 10 weeks

Copying the setup work from Prudix Commerce saves roughly 3–4 weeks of plumbing. As of 4 Oct 2026, weeks 1–9 are built.

How we guarantee the cost limit

Every AI feature has a monthly limit per plan. When a store reaches a limit, the feature keeps working in a simpler non-AI mode instead of turning off. For example, the gift finder still shows matching products, with a standard reason line instead of an AI-written one. That way AI cost can never run past the numbers on this page, and shoppers never see a broken widget.

Features

Tap a feature to see how it works. Tags show the lowest plan that includes it, and whether it uses AI.

Finding the gift

1

AI Gift Finder

Shopper answers 4 quick questions and gets 3–5 real products from the store, each with one line explaining why.

StarterAI▼
What the shopper sees
  • A "Find a gift" button or floating bubble.
  • Questions: who it's for, the occasion, budget, 2–3 words that describe them. Under 30 seconds.
  • 3–5 different products (not near-copies), each with a short honest reason.
  • "None of these": one follow-up question, then fresh picks.
How it works
  • When the app is installed, AI reads each product once and writes a "gift profile": who it suits, which occasions, what kind of person.
  • Shopper answers are matched against those profiles. Products outside the budget are never shown.
  • AI picks the best few and writes the reasons, using only facts about the product.
  • Stores with fewer than ~60 products skip the matching step, and the AI looks at the whole catalog directly.
AI cost: uses AI generations: 1 to 4 per search, depending on the AI option (Standard, Advanced or Premium) the merchant picks (see Plans)Reuse: storefront widget, security checks and budget counters from the Commerce AI Concierge
2

Catalog control and test area

The merchant decides which products the gift finder can suggest, fixes any AI profile, and tries the finder before shoppers do.

StarterAI▼
What the merchant gets
  • "Analyzing your catalog… 340 of 1,200" progress on install.
  • A product list with each gift profile. Edit tags, or hide a product from suggestions.
  • Choose which collections count, for stores larger than the plan's product limit.
  • A test screen: answer the questions as a shopper and see exactly what they'd get.
How it works
  • Products stay in sync automatically when the merchant edits them.
  • Only products whose text changed are re-read by AI, overnight, at half price (bulk processing).
  • Price and stock changes update instantly with no AI cost.
AI cost: about $0.0015 per product readReuse: product sync pattern from Commerce

Storefront placements

Everywhere the gift experience can appear on the store, with no code needed from the merchant.

Starter▼
Options
  • Floating "Find a gift" bubble on every page. It moves itself above other apps' floating buttons (chat, AI assistants) in the same corner, and the merchant can raise it further.
  • "Find a gift" button the merchant drops into the homepage or a collection page.
  • "This is a gift" and "Add to registry" on product pages; "Add a gift note" on the cart page.

Shopper's own language

Starter▼

Preparing the gift

5

AI gift notes

A ready-written note about the actual product, the person and the occasion. The shopper edits it or writes their own.

StarterAI▼
What the shopper sees
  • A note box already filled in, like "Happy 60th, Mom. I thought of you the moment I saw this…".
  • Buttons to rewrite (up to 3 times), edit, or clear it.
  • If they skipped the gift finder, two quick taps (who and occasion) come first.
What the merchant controls
  • Tone: warm and casual, minimal and elegant, playful, or formal.
  • Words the AI must never use, and a maximum length that fits their cards.
  • Notes are never sent automatically. The shopper always sees and approves them.
AI cost: uses AI generations: 1 to 4 per note, depending on the model (each rewrite counts)Reuse: AI safety checks and Brand Profile tone idea from Commerce
6

Several gifts in one order

Handles both "ship it straight to them" and "ship it all to me, I'll hand out gifts to Mom, Dad and the kids".

Starter▼
What the shopper sees
  • One question: "Where is this going? Straight to the recipient / To me, I'll hand them out."
  • Sent to the recipient: the whole order is one gift with one note.
  • Sent to themselves: each item is labelled with who it's for ("Mom", "Dad"), and each gift gets its own note, wrap and card.
  • Items found through the gift finder are labelled automatically, because the finder already asked who it's for.
What the merchant sees
  • Each item in the order shows "Gift for: Mom" in normal Shopify order pages and packing slips.
  • One printed card per gift.
AI cost: none (notes counted under feature 5)Reuse: order webhook handling from Commerce
7

Gift wrap and greeting cards

Up to 3 wrap styles (wrap, bag or box) and 2 greeting cards, with prices and photos, chosen per gift.

Starter▼
How it works
  • The merchant sets names, types (wrap, bag, box, greeting card), photos and prices.
  • Wrap and cards are never pre-selected and the price is always shown. Shopify requires the shopper to choose any extra charge.
  • The app creates one hidden "Gift wrap" product, so wrap and cards show up as normal paid lines in cart and checkout.
  • Shoppers can also add wrap or a card for the whole order from the cart page or cart drawer.
  • Products tagged no-gift-wrap (oversized, digital) never offer wrap.
  • Removing a gift from the cart also removes its wrap and card.
  • The order is tagged so staff can filter orders that need wrapping.
AI cost: noneReuse: product-writing helpers from Commerce
8

Arrive-by dates

The shopper picks when the gift should arrive, and the order is held until the right shipping day.

Growth▼
What the shopper sees
  • A date picker labelled "Arrive by (estimated)". Only dates that can realistically be met are selectable.
How it works
  • The merchant sets processing days, typical delivery days, shipping weekdays, holidays and a daily cutoff time.
  • The app works out a "ship by" date and puts the order on hold until then, then releases it automatically.
  • Items shipped by a third-party warehouse (3PL) aren't held; the order is only tagged with the ship-by date. This keeps us to one fulfillment permission.
  • The order is tagged with the ship-by date so staff can plan.
  • We can't promise carrier delivery, so the wording always says "estimated".
AI cost: noneReuse: background jobs and scheduled tasks from Commerce

Printable gift cards and tags

Starter▼
How it works

  • Print cards for many selected orders at once from the orders list.
  • Card sizes: 4×6 in, A6, and a small tag.
  • Prices are never printed on cards.
AI cost: noneReuse: none (new Shopify admin extension)

Gift receipt (hide prices)

The shopper asks for prices to be hidden, and staff see a clear "no prices" flag.

Starter▼
How it works
  • A checkbox in the gift panel.
  • The order is tagged "Gift receipt", and staff print a price-free gift packing slip from the same print menu as the gift cards (feature 9). No template or code changes for the merchant.
AI cost: none
11

Voice and video messages

The shopper records a message. The recipient scans a QR code on the card to listen or watch. Voice on Growth, voice and video on Pro.

Growth: voicePro: video▼
What the shopper sees
  • "Record a voice note" next to the note box (Growth and Pro), up to 2 minutes.
  • "Record a video" as well on Pro, up to 60 seconds.
  • After launch: "Record after checkout" from the order confirmation page (needs Shopify to approve network access for that extension).
How it works
  • Before recording, the shopper sees: "Anyone with the QR code or link can watch or listen to this."
  • Recordings are stored in cheap cloud storage (Cloudflare R2), behind private links that search engines can't find.
  • The QR code goes on the printed card (feature 9) and can also go on Shopify's own packing slips with a copy-paste snippet (useful for 3PLs). It opens a private page on the store's own domain with the store's name.
  • Merchants see usage, plays and deletion dates on a Voice & video page.
  • Recordings are deleted automatically after a set time (default 90 days after delivery).
  • Recordings for abandoned carts are deleted after 7 days.
Cost: about $0.0005 per voice note, $0.002 per video (storage and processing)Reuse: none (new)
12

Recipient's choice (after launch)

Moved out of v1 on 2026-10-04: most catalogs have no size or color to choose, and the gift receipt already covers exchanges. Built later if apparel and shoe stores ask for it.

Pro▼
What happens
  • The buyer ticks "Let them choose size or color".
  • After checkout, the buyer gets a private link to send to the recipient. We don't send emails.
  • The recipient opens it, sees the gift without a price, and picks a size or color.
  • The app updates the order automatically, then releases it for shipping.
Rules that keep it simple
  • Only options at the same price, so no refunds or extra charges.
  • A deadline (default 5 days). If nobody chooses, the buyer's pick ships.
  • Only works before the order is shipped.
  • Only offered when the shopper pays in the store's own currency. Shopify can't edit orders paid in another currency, so the option is hidden for those shoppers.
AI cost: noneReuse: webhook and job patterns from Commerce

Registries

13

Gift registries with AI suggestions

Shoppers build wedding, baby or birthday lists, share them, and guests buy from them.

ProAI▼
What shoppers see
  • "Add to registry" on product pages (account login required).
  • A shareable registry page showing what's still wanted and what's already bought; guests add items to their cart from it.
  • An owner page to set the occasion and date, change quantities and copy the share link.
  • Guests enter the delivery address in Shopify's normal checkout, so the app never sees or stores addresses.
  • AI suggestions for the owner, picked from the store's own products and cached for the day.
  • We keep only Shopify's customer id for the owner: no names, emails or addresses.
Why merchants care
  • Every shared registry brings new shoppers to the store.
  • A registry report shows new visitors and sales from shared lists.
AI cost: uses AI generations, the same as a gift searchReuse: matching engine from feature 1

Not in this release

  • Corporate and bulk gifting and group gifting: small, unproven demand; the most work to build; and they need names and addresses, which triggers Shopify's stricter customer-data review. If merchants ask for corporate gifting, a light first version ("paste a list of names and hints, AI writes a note for each, print the cards") needs no addresses and can ship as an update.
  • After launch, if merchants ask (from the 2026-10-04 competitor cross-check, docs/COMPETITIVE_CROSSCHECK.md): recipient's choice, recording a message after checkout, gift options inside Shopify checkout (Plus), shipping one order to several addresses, and sending the note by SMS or email (needs recipient contact data, so a customer-data decision first).
  • Permanently out of scope, as in the spec: standalone gift cards, loyalty/rewards, and free-gift or BOGO promotions.

How AI options and generations work

  • The merchant picks an AI option in Settings: Standard, Advanced or Premium, from those their plan includes. Each plan has a default: Standard on Starter, Advanced on Growth, Premium on Pro. Plans and the App Store listing name only the options; we choose (and can upgrade) the model behind each. For transparency, Settings shows what each option currently runs on for that store (e.g. "Premium: currently runs on Claude Sonnet 5").
  • Every AI gift search, AI note and registry suggestion uses generations. Stronger models use more generations per use, in proportion to what they cost us.
  • So a merchant can trade volume for quality: 1,750 generations on Growth is 1,750 searches or notes on Standard, 875 on Advanced, or 437 on Premium (Pro).
  • Reading the catalog doesn't use generations. It runs in the background on our cheapest model (GPT-6 Luna today), within the plan's product limit.
  • Every model on this list must pass the week-2 quality test for gift picks. A model that fails isn't offered.

What happens at a limit

  • AI generations: the gift finder keeps working with matched products and a standard reason ("Fits your $50 budget · cozy and practical"), and notes switch to ready-made templates in the merchant's tone.
  • Products: only the collections the merchant picked are suggested.
  • Voice and video: the recording option is hidden until next month, and the merchant sees an upgrade prompt.

7-day free trial, like Prudix Commerce

  • Why 7 days, not 3: setup (catalog read, placing the button, wrap styles) takes a day or two, and smaller stores need several days of traffic before the merchant sees a real gift-finder sale.
  • What's included: all features and models of the chosen plan, with a smaller trial allowance (table below), like Prudix Commerce's per-plan trial generations. Full plan limits unlock on the first paid day, and the rest of the catalog is analyzed right away.
  • At a trial limit: the same fallbacks as a paid plan, so the storefront never breaks during the trial.
  • One trial per store: uninstalling and reinstalling doesn't give a new trial. This is the same check Prudix Commerce already uses.

Things that move these numbers

  • Shopify's revenue share: Shopify takes 0% of your first $1M in app revenue on its reduced revenue-share plan (check your Partner terms), then 15%. The main tables assume 0%.
  • Hosting: under about 30 merchants, the fixed ~$70/month is split across fewer stores, so the first months run below these margins. At 100+ merchants hosting drops under $1 per merchant and margins rise to about 88–90%.
  • Bigger AI calls than estimated: week-2 measurements are now in these tables; if real traffic runs higher, we adjust the generation weights or limits (they're settings, as in Prudix Commerce) to stay above 80%.
  • Realistic usage is usually 10–30% of limits, and many merchants will pick cheaper models, which puts real margins around 90–95%. The worst case is what's shown above.

Shopify review check

Checked against Shopify's current App Store review requirements and customer-data rules (27 Sep 2026). The fixes are already built into the features above.

Customer data: Level 1 only, same as Prudix Commerce

No feature reads or stores shopper names, emails, phone numbers or addresses. Addresses are only entered in Shopify's own checkout. Registries store only Shopify's customer ID. Level 1 means: collect only what's needed, tell merchants what we process, set retention periods, and encrypt data. Commerce already does all of this.

PermissionWhy we need it
read_products, write_productsRead the catalog; create the hidden gift-wrap product
read_orders, write_ordersAdd gift details and tags to orders
write_merchant_managed_fulfillment_ordersHold orders until the ship-by date
read_themesCheck the widget is switched on (Commerce has this too)

Rules we designed around

  • Extra charges need shopper consent: wrap is never pre-selected, and its price is always shown.
  • No app branding on checkout pages: the record-after-checkout block is unbranded.
  • No manual code edits for merchants: the gift receipt prints from our print menu instead of a template change.
  • Only factual claims: AI reasons may cite only real product facts, and prices in them are checked automatically.
  • Shopper data visible in Shopify admin: notes, dates and message links are saved on the order.
  • Store speed: the app may lower the store's speed score by at most 10 points (needed for the "Built for Shopify" badge, which also needs 50 paid installs and 5 reviews, so it's a later goal). We measure this before submitting.

Decisions

All decided (27 Sep 2026). Prices can be revisited later without code changes.

  • Stack: FastAPI + React, copied from Prudix Commerce.
  • 15 features in one release; corporate and group gifting left out.
  • Plans $19 / $49 / $99 a month (monthly billing only), with model access and AI generations set up like Prudix Commerce.
  • 7-day free trial with per-plan limits; one trial per store.
  • Several gifts per order: "straight to them" or "to me".
  • Voice messages on Growth, video on Pro.
  • No holds for 3PL-shipped items.
